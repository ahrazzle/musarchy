Omarchy × Muse

Your laptop,
reachable from your Muse.

Musarchy connects an Omarchy laptop to your Muse. Pair once in the Muse app. Then your Muse can run commands on the laptop, move files, check its health, list your open windows, and show notifications on your screen. From your phone.

How it works

  1. Install Omarchy 4 on the laptop, from the official ISO.
  2. Run the musarchy installer. It sets up the device agent, creates a locked-down muse account for it, and starts the service.
  3. Pair in the Muse app. Settings → Devices → Developer mode → Add Device. It reconnects on its own after reboots.

What your Muse can do

CommandWhat it does
system.runRun a shell command. Returns stdout, stderr, exit code.
file.read / file.writeRead and write files in chunks.
device.healthUptime, load, memory, disk, temperature.
desktop.runRun a command inside your graphical desktop session.
desktop.windowsList open windows: app, title, workspace, focus.
desktop.notifyShow a notification on your screen.

Not a fork

Musarchy is a layer, not a fork. Omarchy stays stock. Meta's device SDK stays pinned to one upstream commit. Musarchy is the glue between them: an installer ported to Arch, a plugin that adds the desktop commands, and a hardened service setup.

Security in one paragraph

Your Muse gets the permissions of the muse account: no sudo, no password login, no access to your files. There is no command allowlist. If you ask it to do something destructive as that account, it can. Pair on a network you trust. The full model is in docs/SECURITY.md.
